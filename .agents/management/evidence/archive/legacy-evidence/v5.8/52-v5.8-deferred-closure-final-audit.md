## What Was Done

This session closed all V5.8 deferred items:

### 1. EntityPersister Lifecycle Wiring
- `EntityPersister::insert()` fires creating/saving before SQL, created/saved after success, failedToSave on exception
- `EntityPersister::update()` fires updating/saving before SQL, updated/saved after success, failedToSave on exception
- `EntityPersister::delete()` fires deleting before SQL, deleted after success, failedToDelete on exception
- Superset handling verified: saving includes creating/updating, saved includes created/updated
- Exception bubbling: listener failures don't swallow original exceptions

### 2. QueryOrchestrator Lifecycle Wiring
- `QueryOrchestrator::query()` fires executing before SQL, executed after success, failed on exception, slow when threshold met
- `QueryOrchestrator::execute()` fires executing before SQL, executed after success, failed on exception
- No-listener path: registry returns empty list immediately, zero overhead

### 3. Transaction Lifecycle Registry Integration
- `Transaction::begin()` fires TransactionBeginning on outermost
- `Transaction::commit()` fires TransactionCommitted and AfterCommit on outermost (after callbacks run)
- `Transaction::rollback()` fires TransactionRolledBack and AfterRollback on outermost (after callbacks run)
- Nested transactions (savepoints) do not fire lifecycle events
